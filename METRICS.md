# Product Metrics

## North Star Metric

### Completed AI Audits Per Week

The most important metric for this product is the number of completed audits per week.

This metric matters because:
- it directly measures user engagement
- it reflects real product usage
- it creates opportunities for lead conversion
- it indicates whether distribution channels are working

A completed audit represents a user reaching the core value moment of the product.

---

# Input Metrics

## 1. Landing Page Conversion Rate

Definition:
Percentage of visitors who start an audit after visiting the landing page.

Why it matters:
If conversion is low, the messaging, trust, or perceived value may not be strong enough.

---

## 2. Audit Completion Rate

Definition:
Percentage of users who finish the audit after starting it.

Why it matters:
A low completion rate could indicate:
- friction in the form
- unclear instructions
- weak UX
- excessive input requirements

---

## 3. Consultation Request Rate

Definition:
Percentage of completed audits that result in Credex consultation requests or lead submissions.

Why it matters:
This measures actual business value generation rather than simple traffic.

---

# First Instrumentation Priorities

The first analytics events I would track are:

- landing page visits
- audit started
- audit completed
- lead submitted
- consultation requested
- share link generated

These events would help identify:
- drop-off points
- strongest acquisition channels
- highest-intent users

---

# Pivot Threshold

I would seriously reconsider the product direction if:

- audit completion rates remain below 20%
OR
- consultation conversion remains below 1%

after several weeks of active iteration and distribution.

That would suggest:
- users do not trust the recommendations
- the pain point is weaker than expected
- or the product positioning is incorrect

---

# Long-Term Success Signals

Strong signs that the product is succeeding would include:

- users sharing audits publicly
- founders using the tool repeatedly
- growing inbound consultation requests
- referrals from startup communities
- improving conversion efficiency over time

The most important signal would be whether users trust the recommendations enough to make real purchasing decisions based on them.
