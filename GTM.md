# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User

The primary target user is:
- an engineering manager,
- startup founder,
- or technical team lead

at a startup with:
- 5–50 employees
- heavy AI tool adoption
- increasing monthly SaaS and API costs

The ideal customer is already paying for multiple AI products such as:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini
- OpenAI API usage

These teams often move quickly and adopt tools without periodically auditing whether the spending still makes sense.

---

# Trigger Moment

The ideal moment for discovery is when:
- AI invoices increase unexpectedly
- founders review SaaS spending
- engineering teams discuss budget reductions
- companies scale from individual plans into team plans

Typical searches could include:
- “reduce ChatGPT team costs”
- “best AI coding tools pricing”
- “Claude vs ChatGPT pricing”
- “AI SaaS cost optimization”
- “startup AI tool spend”

# First 100 Users Strategy

To acquire the first 100 users with no paid budget, I would:

## 1. Post comparison content
Examples:
- “How much our startup overspent on AI tools”
- “5 common AI subscription mistakes”
- “How much ChatGPT + Claude + Cursor actually costs per developer”

These posts work well on:
- LinkedIn
- X
- Indie Hackers
- Reddit

## 2. Launch on Product Hunt
The tool is naturally suited for Product Hunt because:
- it solves a real startup pain point
- the output is easy to screenshot and share
- the value is immediate

## 3. Founder outreach
Direct outreach to:
- startup founders
- CTOs
- indie hackers

with free personalized audits could generate early traction.

## 4. Referral loop
Shareable audit URLs create organic growth because teams compare spending internally.

# Unfair Distribution Advantage

Credex already operates in the AI infrastructure and credits ecosystem.

That gives a strong distribution advantage because:
- users already interested in reducing AI costs are natural leads

- Credex can immediately monetize high-savings users

The audit itself becomes both:
- a useful standalone product
- and a lead-generation channel
