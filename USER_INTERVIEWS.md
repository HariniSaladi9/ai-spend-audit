# User Interviews

## Interview 1 — Priya (Student)

### Background
Priya is a student who regularly uses AI tools for studying, coding help, and general questions.

### Key Quotes

> “I use ChatGPT for coding, studying, and doubts regarding anything.”

> “Sometimes I feel confused about subscriptions.”

> “I didn’t know a website could exist to check AI tool subscriptions.”

### Most Surprising Insight

The most surprising insight was that awareness itself is a problem. Priya was unfamiliar with the idea of auditing AI tool spending or comparing subscriptions systematically.

### What It Changed About The Product

This interview reinforced the importance of:
- keeping the product simple,
- explaining value clearly,
- and not assuming users already understand AI pricing optimization.

It also highlighted the need for educational onboarding.

## Interview 2 — Hanvitha (Student)

### Background

Hanvitha currently uses Gemini AI on the free plan for:
- coding,
- studying,
- and casual conversations.

### Key Quotes

> “I currently use Gemini AI.”

> “We don’t need subscriptions because we don’t use it that much.”

> “I never used any website like that.”

### Most Surprising Insight

The interview showed that many users remain entirely on free plans and may not initially see themselves as needing cost optimization tools.

### What It Changed About The Product

This feedback suggested the product should:
- support both free and paid users,
- explain upgrade decisions,
- and provide educational comparisons even when direct savings are small.

It also reinforced that trust and simplicity matter more than aggressive upselling.

## Interview 3 — Vijay (Student Developer)

### Background

Vijay uses Claude AI and previously purchased a ChatGPT subscription for coding-related help and technical questions.

### Key Quotes

> “I already bought ChatGPT subscription but I never used ChatGPT.”

> “Now I changed to use Claude.”

> “We generally use AI for coding doubts.”

### Most Surprising Insight

The most interesting insight was that users sometimes subscribe impulsively before fully understanding whether the tool matches their workflow.

This creates wasted spending and overlapping subscriptions.

### What It Changed About The Product

This conversation reinforced the importance of:
- recommendation accuracy,
- highlighting overlapping tools,
- and helping users avoid unnecessary duplicate subscriptions.

It also validated the idea that users may switch AI tools frequently based on perceived usefulness.
