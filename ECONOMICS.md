# Unit Economics

## Estimated Lead Value

If Credex deploys this tool successfully, a converted customer could be highly valuable because AI infrastructure spending is recurring.

Assume:
- average startup AI spend identified through the tool: $1,000/month
- Credex helps reduce costs by 20%
- Credex captures part of that value through discounted infrastructure credits

If Credex earns approximately:
- $150/month gross profit per converted customer

then annual customer value becomes:

$150 × 12 = $1,800/year

If the average customer remains active for 2 years, estimated lifetime value becomes:

$1,800 × 2 = $3,600 LTV

## Organic Content
Examples:
- Product Hunt launch
- X threads
- LinkedIn posts
- Reddit discussions

Estimated CAC:
$10–$40 per qualified lead

This channel is low-cost but depends heavily on strong distribution and shareability.

## Founder Outreach

Direct outreach to founders and engineering managers could produce:
- high-quality leads
- lower conversion volume
- higher conversion intent

Estimated CAC:
$50–$120 per qualified lead

This is more labor-intensive but can work well early-stage.

# Conversion Funnel

Example funnel assumptions:

## Step 1 — Audit Completed
1,000 users complete audits

## Step 2 — Consultation Request
5% request consultation

1,000 × 5% = 50 consultations

## Step 3 — Paying Customers
20% of consultation users purchase credits

50 × 20% = 10 customers

---

# Revenue Estimate

If:
- each customer generates $1,800 annual value

then:

10 customers × $1,800 = $18,000 annual revenue

from 1,000 audit completions.

# What Would Need To Be True For $1M ARR

To reach $1M ARR in 18 months:

Assume:
- $1,800 annual value per customer

Required customer count:

$1,000,000 ÷ $1,800 ≈ 556 customers

If conversion from audit → customer is ~1%, then:

556 customers requires approximately:

55,600 completed audits

over 18 months.

That equals roughly:
- 3,100 audits/month
- 100 audits/day

This becomes realistic only if:
- the audit tool is highly shareable
- SEO and community distribution succeed
- the recommendation quality builds trust
- Credex closes consultations efficiently

---

# Biggest Economic Risks

The largest risks are:

## 1. Low intent users
Many users may use the tool for curiosity without real purchasing intent.

## 2. Weak viral sharing
If users do not share audit links publicly, growth becomes slower and CAC rises.

## 3. Pricing volatility
AI vendor pricing changes rapidly, which can make recommendations outdated.

## 4. Trust barriers
Financial recommendations require credibility. If users doubt the calculations, conversion rates may fall significantly.

---

# Why The Model Can Still Work

Despite these risks, the economics are attractive because:
- AI spending is growing rapidly
- companies increasingly adopt multiple overlapping tools
- savings opportunities are often recurring
- even a small percentage of converted leads can produce meaningful revenue

The audit tool functions both as:
- a useful product
- and a scalable lead-generation engine.
